HI25 ladders: ideal and ambipolar

Two resolution ladders of the HI25 driven-turbulence deck, each climbing 256³ → 512³ → 1024³ → 2048³ on the same rung times as the v5 ladder. One is ideal MHD and one adds ISM ambipolar diffusion (AD) from t = 0. Both use the new low-β energy treatment (the "1% rule") from the start, so the H2 history at every rung is made with the final scheme.

Ambipolar diffusion

The same deck with ISM ambipolar diffusion on from t = 0: resistivity from C⁺ and H⁺ collisions with H, H2 and He in the H2 closure, super-time-stepped (RKL2) and Strang-split around the ideal step. Starting AD at t = 0 keeps the H2 history consistent with the AD physics.

Storage is the other constraint. The project quota is 30 TB shared with another user, and 7.3 TB is free now. The old 1024³ runs are deleted when the new 1024³ rungs finish, and the old 2048³ run, its painted t = 2.5 state and its seed (4.3 TB) once the new 2048³ rungs are running on schedule.

Why the ladders are being redone

  • The scheme changed. The old ladder used CT-Poynting + FOFC with an entropy floor at 0.97. The new scheme removes most of the numerical cold tail at low β: at 512³ it cuts the mass below 5 K about fourfold against production while matching its bulk statistics (gates G1–G5).
  • H2 remembers. H2 forms over about 11 Myr at nH = 100 cm⁻³ and 35 Myr at 30 cm⁻³, comparable to or longer than a rung. Restarting only the top rungs would leave most of the top rung's H2 made by the old scheme.
  • Strong scaling makes the early rungs cheap, so the ladder can be climbed quickly once the configuration is frozen.

Mass fractions are window means; the last column is the maximum over each arm's whole run. AD costs 2.0 (midpoint) to 2.6 (point) times the ideal step at 256³, and the ratio is expected to grow with resolution.

What AD did by t = 2.5: about 5% less magnetic power at k ≳ 30 (scales below ~0.8 pc) and 2% less ⟨J²⟩; temperature, entropy and bulk statistics unchanged. The implied ion–neutral drift, from the code's ISM coefficient with H⁺ included, has a volume median of 0.017 km/s and a maximum near 1 km/s, largest in the warm gas. The implied AD heating is 4.0% of the driving power without AD and 3.3–3.5% with it. These numbers should grow with resolution (heating roughly 2.5× per doubling until the AD scale is resolved), which is why both ladders go on and the AD one is reviewed at 512³.

Decisions and timeline

  • Done: 256³ and 512³ rungs of both ladders (midpoint AD, ncontrol=32 in both ladders so fused Strang can join half-steps).
  • Nov 24, first 1024³ attempt failed. Refining 512³ to 1024³ on 16 ranks overflowed an MPI send buffer, the script took the linked 512³ backup for the refine stage's output, and sbatch cut the output list at its first comma. The refine stage now gets a larger buffer (ncachemax 4.4M, as the v4 ladder needed), the script ignores linked backups and checks the restart level and the output list, and both rungs were resubmitted at 22:20. About 1.5 h lost.
  • Next: when the sizing test reports, set the 2048³ GPU counts and queue both 2048³ rungs with afterok dependencies on the 1024³ rungs.
  • Review of the AD ladder: the implied heating fraction (~3.5% of the driving at 256³) and the small-scale smoothing should grow with resolution; to be measured from the 512³ and 1024³ outputs, together with the short heat-placement replay (midpoint vs point vs AD off) both reviewers asked for.
  • Top rung: paint tracers and dyes at the 2048³ designated backup (t = 2.5), as before, using the prod-joint build.

Configuration

Ideal step: the 1% rule (namelist) 
  ct_poynting=3            ! Eq. 15: face-state induction correction
  fofc=.false.
  fofc_smin=0.0d0
  ct_edge_fofc=.true.      ! first-order fallback on edges
  ct_coupled_fofc=.true.   ! coupled face/edge fallback, Eq. 15 energy
  ct_fofc_passes=8
  ct_fofc_entropy=.true.   ! entropy sensor, relaxed rule (1% cap)

Flag a cell if its specific entropy falls below max[(1 − r) smin, smin − Δi] minus rounding, with r = 0.01 and smin the 27-cell minimum of the old state.

Non-ideal step: ISM ambipolar diffusion (namelist) 
  eta_ad=0.0d0
  nimhd_solver='sts'
  nimhd_eta_model='ism'
  nimhd_ct_field='midpoint'      ! chosen over 'point'
  nimhd_split='strang_fused'     ! fused Strang; required for the fused Itô-2 tracers

midpoint conserves total energy to roundoff but can lower a cell's thermal energy where AD smooths the field between its faces at low β. point clips the AD heating to be nonnegative cell by cell, at a small cost in energy conservation, and is about 25% slower here.

Code and builds
  • Base: paris cnm-opt 14b89744 (Itô-2 tracers and H2 dye, fused Strang AD, CNM fast paths).
  • Method: 5 commits on top, branch ct-int5-on-cnm-opt (ad9e78ea), not yet pushed.
  • Builds: cnm-opt's production make arguments, prod-gas (NPSCAL=1) for the ladder rungs and prod-joint (NPSCAL=2, 64-bit positions, fused-AD tracers) for the painted top rung. NVHPC 24.7, sm_90.
  • The 1% rule runs on the generic UCT path; cnm-opt's CNM fast paths serve only the production P3 scheme. At 512³ per GPU on H100, production P3 takes 0.20 s per step and the 1% rule 0.295 s.
  • Memory at 512³ per GPU on H100: production P3 58 GiB, the 1% rule 69 GiB, the 1% rule with midpoint AD more than 80 GB.
Reference costs from the v5 ladder (production scheme)
  • 1024³ on 8 H100: about 0.42 s per step; 1.75 → 2.25 is about 190k steps (2.25 → 2.5 took 96k).
  • 2048³ on 64 H100 (8 nodes), the corrected-CNM run: 220.8k steps for 2.25 → 2.5 at 0.251 s per step (production P3 with the CNM fast paths).
  • 256³ with the 1% rule: 0.044–0.049 s per step on one GPU; the earlier 256³ run reached t = 2.75 in 2.75 h on a B200.
