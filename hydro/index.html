    01 Research / particle-in-cell dust

    Dust, one grain at a time

    Interstellar dust as magnetohydrodynamic particle-in-cell superparticles: aerodynamic drag, the Lorentz force, and momentum fed back into the gas — integrated on the drift velocity so the timestep never has to resolve a Larmor orbit or a stopping time.

    [this is where you talk about the MHD-PIC dust method in RAMSES — the physical problem it was built for, why the drift-velocity formulation was the key idea, and what surprised you about the results]

    The method

    • Grains carried as massive superparticles with drag and Lorentz force, with self-consistent back-reaction on the gas.
    • Operator split on the dust–gas drift velocity: the Lorentz operator is second-order, symplectic and A-stable; the drag integrators are L-stable.
    • Three drag laws — constant stopping rate, Epstein, and Epstein with the supersonic Baines correction.
    • CIC and TSC deposition; constrained transport and an HLLD Riemann solver underneath.
    • Validated on damped gyromotion, Mach 3 and Mach 100 dusty shock tubes, and circularly polarised dusty Alfvén waves.

    02 Research / dual-frame moment method

    The dual-frame moment method

    A variational phase-space moment scheme with manifest realizability: Lagrangian geometry carried on an Eulerian grid, closed so that the numerical state can never leave the space of physically possible distributions — and so that the scheme reports exactly where its own closure is failing.

    [this is where you talk about what the dual-frame moment method is and why you and Tom Abel started building it — the problem with single-frame moment closures for cold, multi-streaming dust, and what "manifest realizability" buys you]

    Verified so far

    • Cold-limit unification. The variational action's cold limit matches the analytic Zel'dovich solution to 2.6 × 10⁻⁸, and the Hessian degeneracy localises the caustic where theory says it should be.
    • Exact passive-scalar transport. Tracer change is structurally zero — L∞ = 0.0 — against 25–114 cells of smearing for Eulerian upwind on the same problem.
    • Action-based refinement. 37.8 per cent fewer cells than gradient-based AMR at slightly better L² error, with mass, momentum and tracers conserved bit-exactly across refine/coarsen.
    • Symplectic integration in the Cholesky sector of the second-moment cone, with a realizability projection that keeps long runs alive.

    03 Research / non-ideal MHD

    When the field slips

    Molecular gas is barely ionised, so the magnetic field is only partly attached to it. Ambipolar diffusion and Ohmic dissipation on GPUs — advanced by Runge–Kutta–Gegenbauer super-time-stepping and an L-stable implicit constrained-transport solver, at the ideal-MHD timestep.

    [this is where you talk about non-ideal MHD — why ambipolar diffusion matters for star formation and for dust, and why the parabolic timestep is the thing that kills you]

    The numerics

    • Ambipolar diffusion runs at the ideal CFL step: stiffness sets the super-time-stepping stage count, never the hydro timestep. Measured stiffness ratios up to ≈ 44 in production 256³ runs.
    • An L-stable [0/2] Padé implicit constrained-transport solver, matrix-free BiCGStab with a squared shifted-diffusion preconditioner that makes the condition number stiffness-independent (≤ 1.17) — Krylov work collapses to single digits.
    • Fused RKL2 kernel: −40 % on the matched ambipolar timer on an A100, agreeing with the unfused reference to L₁ = 2 × 10⁻⁷.
    • Convergence measured on a 32³ → 256³ circularly polarised Alfvén-wave study: order 2.50 (L₁), damping rate γfit/γ → 1.000.
    • Underneath: upwind constrained transport with an HLLD Riemann solver, and EMF reflux across AMR levels.

    04 Research / cosmic rays

    Cosmic rays, self-confined

    Relativistic particles trapped by the waves they themselves grow. I follow them as superparticles inside RAMSES, and recast gyroresonant pitch-angle scattering as a stochastic differential equation so that quasi-linear transport can be integrated rather than assumed.

    [this is where you talk about cosmic ray confinement — the streaming instability, why the effective diffusion coefficient is the whole ballgame for galactic feedback, and what you are testing with particle-in-cell runs]

    What exists

    • The RAMSES MHD-PIC module built for charged dust, extended to relativistic cosmic rays with a relativistic Boris pusher and a reduced speed of light.
    • 128³ streaming-instability experiments on Stellar: CR-to-gas mass ratio 10⁻⁹, c̃/c = 6 × 10⁻⁴, plasma β = 1, γ₀ = 10 — with measured growth of density, magnetic and velocity fluctuations.
    • Pitch-angle scattering written as an SDE with quasi-linear Fokker–Planck coefficients, so scattering is integrated alongside the orbit instead of being imposed.
    • Co-author on Cosmic ray and plasma coupling for isothermal supersonic turbulence in the magnetised interstellar medium (Sampson, Beattie, Teyssier, Kempski, Moseley, Commerçon, Dubois & Rosdahl), arXiv:2506.03768 — a two-moment CRMHD study of coupled and decoupled regimes.

    05 Code / phrike

    phrike

    φρίκη — a shudder. A Fourier pseudo-spectral solver for compressible Euler and non-ideal MHD that runs identically on NumPy and on a GPU, and that advances the stiff diffusion with exponential-in-time operators instead of sub-stepping through it.

    There is a small relative of it running in your browser: Shocks — the isothermal Euler equations solved conservatively with a Godunov scheme, driven into supersonic turbulence, with the density PDF measured live. Not spectral, and not phrike, but the same instinct.

    [this is where you talk about why you wrote phrike — what you wanted that existing codes would not give you, and what it is for now]

    Design

    • Fourier collocation with pointwise physical-space fluxes, 2/3-rule dealiasing and a high-order exponential modal filter; a Legendre–Gauss–Lobatto basis is available in 1D for non-periodic boundaries.
    • Constant Ohmic diffusion is exact in Fourier space. Variable-η Ohmic and ambipolar diffusion use frozen-coefficient matrix-exponential action, or a second-order L-stable rational resolvent solved matrix-free by preconditioned conjugate gradients.
    • Embedded adaptive Runge–Kutta (RK23 / RK45 / RK78) with PI step-size control, or plain RK2/RK4 under a wave-speed CFL.
    • Solenoidal projection of B every step, so ∇·B stays at roundoff.
    • One duck-typed array backend: NumPy/SciPy FFT, pyFFTW, or PyTorch on CUDA, Apple MPS or CPU — same numerics, single or double precision.
    • Tests assert physics, not fixtures: exact Ohmic damping to roundoff, dt³ one-step error for the resolvent, and dust relaxing to its fluctuation–dissipation equilibrium.

    Scope, stated plainly

    Adaptive in time only — there is no mesh refinement and no MPI. Euler runs in 1D, 2D and 3D; MHD and the dust moment fluid are 1D and 2D with three-component vectors.

    What's going on in the background?

    Colophon. The background is compressible: the isothermal Euler equations solved conservatively with a Godunov scheme on a periodic — grid. The reconstruction is piecewise parabolic — Colella & Woodward's interface interpolation on MonCen-limited slopes, monotonised so the parabola cannot invent an extremum, and averaged over the slab the flow crosses in half a step, which is where the time centring comes from. The flux is HLLC: three waves, so the contact is resolved and the transverse velocity and the dye are taken from whichever side of it the interface lies on rather than blended across it. That last point is not a refinement — with HLL's two waves there is no contact, a shear layer is a tangential discontinuity in exactly those variables, and a Kelvin–Helmholtz layer was being diffused as fast as it could grow. The reference's own configuration — piecewise linear, slope_type = 2, trace2d's predictor — is still in the file, and it is also what runs for a second and a half after anything disturbs the box: a parabola fitted across a freshly injected discontinuity is where PPM is fragile, since the monotonisation acts on each variable independently and a state monotone in every variable separately can still be unphysical taken together. So the page is parabolic while it is quiet and piecewise linear while you are driving it. Parabolic costs about fifteen per cent more per frame and keeps visibly more of the small scales. One flux evaluation per step either way. The update is unsplit and conservative — mass holds to 1.000 over a minute — and cmpdt supplies the Courant floor, recomputed from the current state before every step and reduced on the GPU so nothing has to come back to the CPU. The dye is a passive scalar, which is exactly what the reference does with one. It is the scheme from mini-ramses-ism's GPU hydro path, cut down to two dimensions and to its cheapest configuration. The driving is held transonic — rms Mach about 0.7, so the bulk of the flow is subsonic and keeps its vortices, while the tail of the distribution is supersonic and steepens into fronts. Whirls and shocks, rather than one or the other. The timestep is min(dtdefault, dtCourant): a fixed step chosen a factor of 2.5 inside the stability limit, with cmpdt as a floor that engages only when something — a scroll, a click — makes the flow faster than the state it was chosen for. A fixed number of steps per frame is a fixed cost, so the frame rate is stable, and a fixed step means the picture keeps a constant pace: the same apparent speed as the live site's projection solver, 0.060 box heights per second against 0.057, both measured. Between grid and screen the gas is reconstructed with Catmull-Rom, which interpolates, so a front keeps its position and amplitude while the facets of bilinear go away; the grains gather their gas velocity through a cubic B-spline, the shape function of a particle-in-cell code, so a grain crossing a cell boundary feels no kink and the population cannot print the grid onto its own clustering. The incompressible scheme this page used to run — semi-Lagrangian advection, vorticity confinement, elliptic pressure by a multigrid V-cycle — is still here, at projection. The dust is unchanged from the live site: — Lagrangian grains with stopping times drawn log-uniformly over 1.4 decades, advanced by a first-order implicit (backward Euler) drag, lit by their drift |v − ugas| and sized in proportion to grain radius. What differs is what they are falling through — grains in a compressible flow pile up against shock fronts, which a divergence-free field cannot show at all. Written from scratch in WebGL2, no libraries.
