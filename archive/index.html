Frozen snapshots of this site, kept for reference. Each one is the real page as it stood on that date — its own HTML, CSS and JavaScript, including whatever the background simulation was doing at the time. Images are shared with the live site rather than duplicated, so a snapshot is a few hundred kilobytes rather than nine megabytes.

They are not maintained, and they are marked with a banner so they cannot be mistaken for live.
