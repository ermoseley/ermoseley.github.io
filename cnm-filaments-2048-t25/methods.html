Reading the figures

  • Hydrogen columns sum number density times cell width. The molecular column counts H₂ molecules. The molecular fraction counts the fraction of hydrogen nuclei bound in H₂, so its projection equals twice the molecular column divided by the hydrogen-nuclei column.
  • Density, temperature, thermal pressure, field strength, speed and plasma beta projections weight each cell by gas mass. Each sightline velocity centroid and dispersion uses the same weights. The dispersion measures bulk cell-to-cell velocity variations along the sightline. Cold gas has a reconstructed temperature below 200 K.
  • Each midplane is one native cell thick, at index 1024 (zero-based) and coordinate 12.50610 pc. Horizontal/vertical coordinates are y/z for the x view, x/z for the y view, and x/y for the z view.
  • The Dragonfire temperature scale places black at 1895.77 K. This is the temperature predicted at the center of the uniform initial box with the run's initial pressure, density, and chemistry. The hot and cold log-temperature ranges each occupy half the color scale. The display JSON files in the science folder record the ranges of all palettes.
  • Magnetic strength averages the magnitudes of cell-centered fields. The field-direction overlay combines line-of-sight mass-weighted pseudo-Stokes Q and U, then averages them within image blocks before computing an orientation. Each segment has a 180-degree ambiguity.
  • Magnetic curl, vorticity, divergence and alignment slices use true three-dimensional centered differences with periodic boundaries. Magnetic curl is in µG/pc. Zero-length vectors have undefined alignment. Molecular-column contours use 8 × 8 native pixels per block.
  • Thermochemical cell histograms give fractions of the full gas mass per bin. Density–temperature also has a volume-weighted panel. The sightline histograms weight each map pixel by projected area. Histogram display metadata include the fraction inside each displayed range. The full-volume density–magnetic-curl histograms (mass or volume fraction per square dex) and field–curl alignment PDFs follow the earlier 1024³ analysis. Alignment PDFs are conditional on density and include separate volume and curl-squared weights.

Thermochemistry and provenance

Thermal pressure is reconstructed from total energy after subtracting kinetic and face-averaged magnetic energy. Temperature uses the source-matched H₂/H⁺ mixture, grain recombination and a four-dimensional ionization table. The checkpoint omits the stored-time shielding cache, so the six-ray photoelectric attenuation is reconstructed from the snapshot density. Small differences from the last live cooling update are possible.

The corrected 2048³ snapshot was saved at coarse step 571786 (61.112 Myr). Each cell spans 0.01221 pc. The solver revision and numerical checks are in the snapshot summary.
